# Grant Roadmap (SBIR / STTR)

Not legal or financial advice. Confirm entity, ownership, and export-control
items with counsel or your local SBDC / APEX Accelerator.

## Phase 0: Eligibility and registrations (start immediately)

- [ ] LLC filed and active; EIN letter on hand; business bank account open
- [ ] SAM.gov registration complete (UEI, CAGE) - free, takes weeks
- [ ] Login.gov account created
- [ ] SBIR.gov company profile registered
- [ ] Grants.gov registration (for civilian agencies)
- [ ] IP assignment from founder to company signed
- [ ] Ownership, control, size, and PI eligibility confirmed
- [ ] Export-control (EAR/ITAR) consult completed
- [ ] SBDC / APEX Accelerator appointment booked

## Phase 1: Evidence

- [ ] Wire `benchmarks/targets.py` to the real loop
- [ ] Run benchmarks idle and under load; record results
- [ ] Update `quantified_performance_metrics.md` with measured numbers
- [ ] Fill the capabilities statement placeholders
- [ ] Run `./repo_cleanup.sh`, review, then `--apply`

## Phase 2: Customers and topics

- [ ] Shortlist 2-3 open topics on sbir.gov and DSIP
- [ ] Contact topic authors during the pre-release question window
- [ ] Collect letters of support or LOIs from prospective users or primes

## Phase 3: Proposal

- [ ] Technical volume: objectives, novelty, risk, milestones, pass/fail criteria
- [ ] Commercialization plan with named customer and transition path
- [ ] Cost volume (use `grants/budget_calculator.py` if present)
- [ ] Review by an SBDC advisor before submission
- [ ] Submit one focused Phase I proposal before the deadline
